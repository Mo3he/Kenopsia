Kenopsia

A music player for the music you actually own.

Local files, a NAS in the cupboard, a Subsonic server, iCloud Drive, Backblaze. Add a source once and it all lands in one library.

Free · No ads · No accounts · No tracking

Built for people with a music collection

Not a streaming service. Not a front end for someone else's catalogue. Just a good player for the files you've spent years collecting.

Play from anywhere

Local files, Subsonic and Navidrome, NAS over DLNA, iCloud Drive, Backblaze B2, your Apple Music library, and web radio.

Proper playback

Gapless with optional crossfade, a 10-band parametric EQ, and ReplayGain volume levelling. FLAC, ALAC, WAV and AIFF included.

A library that behaves

Browse by artist, album, genre, playlist or folder. Smart playlists, a tag editor, artwork auto-fetch, and a duplicate finder.

In the car and on your wrist

Full CarPlay browsing with search and Shuffle All, an Apple Watch companion, a Home Screen widget, AirPlay and Chromecast.

Yours, offline

Pin a source for offline listening, or drag files straight in over Wi-Fi from any browser on your network.

Private by design

No account to create. Nothing tracked, nothing sold. Your library stays on your device and credentials live in the iOS Keychain.

Kenopsia is free

No ads, no subscription, no upsell, and nothing harvested from you. It's built and maintained by one person, in evenings and weekends, mostly in response to emails from people using it.

If it's earned a place on your phone, a coffee keeps it going.
